The build

What is an LSF tiny home?

LSF stands for light steel frame. Instead of brick or a repurposed shipping container, an ASAD tiny home is built on an engineered light steel frame, finished in timber and fitted out inside.

  • 01
    Engineered structure
    The light steel frame carries a minimum 30-year structure guarantee, and every unit comes with an engineer’s certificate.
  • 02
    Built to your site
    Every unit is custom-sized to your site, and custom designs and sizes are available.
  • 03
    Delivered and installed
    Delivery and installation are included within 100 km of the Boksburg branch.
  • 04
    Off-grid friendly
    Solar, a septic tank and a back-up water system can be added. See options and prices.

Good to know

Tiny home questions.

How much does a tiny home cost in South Africa?

ASAD tiny homes start from R325 000 excl. VAT for the 25 m² Garden Cottage Living Unit and go up to R504 000 excl. VAT for the 36 m² Roof Top Pod. The right price depends on the design, size and options you choose.

What is included in the price?

Delivery and installation within 100 km of Boksburg, an engineer’s certificate and the electrical COC are included. VAT, connection to mains, other COC certificates, municipal plan approval and council fees, and transport beyond 100 km are not.

Do I need planning permission for a tiny home?

It depends on your municipality, the size of the unit and whether it is permanent. Some small secondary dwellings qualify for simplified approval. We check zoning and building lines with you before you order.

Is there a guarantee on an ASAD tiny home?

Yes. The light steel frame carries a minimum 30-year structure guarantee, and every unit comes with an engineer’s certificate.

Can I rent out a tiny home on Airbnb?

Yes, through long-term letting or short-term platforms like Airbnb. See our Airbnb Guest Suite Pod.
