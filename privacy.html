Privacy policy.

How Asad Projects handles your personal information when you use this website. Last updated 18 November 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Asad Projects (Reg No. 2018/589606/07), Unit 13, Turbo Park, Paul Smith Rd, Anderbolt, Boksburg, Gauteng. We are the responsible party for the personal information described below, in terms of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). You can reach us at info@asad.co.za or 083 371 5807.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, email address, phone number, location, the pod design you are interested in, and any message you write.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: whatever you send us when you contact us directly.
  • Website usage: anonymous statistics about how the site is used, such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location, collected through Google Analytics.
  • Advertising measurement: the Meta (Facebook) Pixel records that you visited the site or sent an enquiry, so we can measure and improve our adverts.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and arrange delivery and installation.
  • To follow up on your quotation.
  • To understand which pages and adverts work, and to improve the website.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the website and our business: our web host (Hostinger), Google (Analytics), Meta (the Pixel) and our email provider. They process information on our behalf or under their own privacy terms. Some of them store data outside South Africa. We may also share information where the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel use cookies and similar technologies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use your browser’s privacy tools, and the website will still work.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry, quotation or build, and for as long as the law requires us to keep business records. You can ask us to delete your details sooner.

How we protect it

The website uses HTTPS, and access to enquiries is limited to people at Asad Projects who need it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information such as ID numbers or banking details through the enquiry form.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may object to us using it. Email info@asad.co.za and we will respond. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
