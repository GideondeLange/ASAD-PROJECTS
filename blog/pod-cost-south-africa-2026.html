Real 2026 prices for every ASAD pod, what changes the price, and what is usually not included.

What changes the price of a pod?

Two pods of the same size can quote differently. These are the four things that move the number:

  • Size. The biggest factor. A 4 m² backyard office and a 100 m² three-bedroom home sit more than twenty times apart.
  • Finishes and fit-out. The bathroom and kitchenette fittings, the interior finish and whether the pod has full electrical all affect the quote. See the finishes and inclusions page for what a standard quotation covers.
  • Add-ons. Anything can be added to any pod: decking from R700/m², a pergola from R900/m², a 5kW solar system at R63 300, a septic tank at R16 000, a hot tub at R35 000, a fireplace at R19 200 or an aircon at R13 000. Add-on prices exclude VAT.
  • Site access and transport. Our standard transport radius is 100 km from our base in Anderbolt, Boksburg. Beyond that, transport is quoted separately. A steep, rocky or remote site can also add steelwork, as we showed on the elevated build at Tamboti River Reserve.

What is usually not included in a pod price?

Across the industry, the pod itself is only part of the project cost. Expect these to be separate from the base price, and make sure any supplier you compare us with tells you the same:

  • Connection to mains — water, electricity and sewer or septic connections on your site.
  • COC certificates — electrical and other compliance certificates.
  • Council approval — plan approval and council fees. See how the process works in our planning and approvals guide.
  • Transport beyond the standard radius — anything outside 100 km of Boksburg.
  • Installation beyond the base build — extras such as decking, pergolas and solar are quoted on top.
  • VAT — all ASAD prices are quoted excluding VAT.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a pod cost in South Africa?

ASAD living pods cost from R45 000 for a backyard office or guard house up to R1 095 000 for the 3 Bedroom Pod. The price depends on size, finishes, add-ons and how far your site is from our Boksburg base.

What is not included in the price of a pod?

ASAD prices exclude VAT, connection to mains services, COC certificates, municipal plan approval and council fees, transport beyond our standard 100 km radius, and installation beyond the base build.

Does the price include delivery?

Transport within a standard 100 km radius of our base in Anderbolt, Boksburg is part of a normal quotation. Outside that radius, transport is quoted separately.
