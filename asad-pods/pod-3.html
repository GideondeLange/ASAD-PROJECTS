Pod 3 is ASAD’s most affordable full-size tiny home — the same 17m² steel-framed footprint, private bathroom, fitted kitchen, and full electrical as Pod 1, at a lower starting price. It’s the option for anyone who wants a genuinely complete tiny home without the higher-spec finish level of the pricier models.

Built on the same durable steel framework as every ASAD pod, Pod 3 is a real, liveable space — not a stripped-down shell. It suits the same range of uses as Pod 1: backyard rental income, a granny flat, or a standalone office with full amenities, for anyone working to a tighter budget.

What's Included

  • 17m² of steel-framed living space
  • Private bathroom with shower, toilet, and vanity
  • Fitted kitchen with prep space and stove
  • Full electrical wiring and fittings throughout
  • Custom sizing available on request

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Pod 3 cost?

Pod 3 starts from R204,000 excluding VAT, with financing available from approximately R4,300 per month — ASAD’s most affordable full-size tiny home.

What’s the difference between Pod 1 and Pod 3?

Both are 17m² steel-framed tiny homes with a bathroom, kitchen, and full electrical. Pod 3 is the more affordable of the two, at a different finish and price point than Pod 1.

Does Pod 3 include a bathroom and kitchen?

Yes. Pod 3 includes a private bathroom, a fitted kitchen, and full electrical wiring as standard.
