The 2 Bedroom Pod is ASAD's full-size off-grid living unit — starting from 85m², with the fully-finished configuration shown here totalling 101.8m² (80.5m² of indoor living space plus 21.3m² of covered decking). It combines durable steel-frame construction with real timber cladding and floors, delivering exceptional value as a garden retreat, guest suite, or income-generating investment property.

Flexible finance is available with 5 and 10-year terms — not only on the pod itself, but on the land as well. Delivery and installation are included within 100km of Gauteng, with additional costs applying beyond this radius.

Floor Plan & Room Breakdown

The configuration shown totals 101.8m², laid out as:

  • Bedroom 1 — 13m²
  • Bedroom 2 — 12m²
  • Bathroom — 7m²
  • Open Kitchen Area — 19m²
  • Lounge Area — 20m²
  • Open Patio 1 — 15m²
  • Open Patio 2 — 6m²
  • External Decking — 21.3m²
  • Total Area — 101.8m²

What's Included

  • 2 bedrooms and a full bathroom
  • Full kitchen with built-in gas stove and custom-designed cabinetry
  • Robust steel structure with real timber cladding and floors
  • 10kW solar system, with delivery and installation included within 100km of Gauteng
  • Floor-to-ceiling steel-framed glazing and doors opening onto the covered deck
  • Custom sizing available on request

Design & Interior

Kitchen — full kitchen with gas stove and custom-designed, any-colour cabinetry.
Bathroom — top-flush toilet, corner shower unit, vanity, and mixer tap.
Wall & ceiling cladding — smooth-finish interior wall and ceiling cladding throughout, with vinyl flooring.
Glazing — floor-to-ceiling steel-framed glazing and doors opening onto the covered deck.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the 2 Bedroom Pod cost?

The 2 Bedroom Pod starts from R980,000 excluding VAT for an 85m² unit. The fully-finished 101.8m² configuration shown, with indoor living space plus covered patios and decking, is priced from R885,000 excluding VAT depending on specification. Final pricing depends on size and finish.

What's included in the 2 Bedroom Pod?

Two bedrooms, a full bathroom, an open-plan kitchen and lounge, and a 10kW solar system with delivery and installation included within 100km of Gauteng. Robust steel structure with real timber cladding throughout.

Is the 2 Bedroom Pod off-grid capable?

Yes. It comes standard with a 10kW solar system, making it suited to off-grid retreats, garden suites, or income-generating rental units on land without existing services.
