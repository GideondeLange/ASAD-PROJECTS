The Entertainment Pod is built for a different job than ASAD’s tiny homes — not a place to live full-time, but a dedicated backyard entertainment space: a braai room, a bar area, or a games room, built on the same durable steel framework as every ASAD pod. It’s also ASAD’s most affordable model.

At 17m² as standard, with kitchenette units available for anyone who wants a wet bar or light catering setup built in, the Entertainment Pod works well as a standalone structure separate from the main house — close enough to use constantly, far enough to feel like its own space.

What's Included

  • 17m² of steel-framed entertainment space
  • Full electrical wiring and fittings throughout
  • Optional kitchenette unit available
  • Custom sizing available on request

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Entertainment Pod cost?

The Entertainment Pod starts from R215,000 excluding VAT — ASAD’s most affordable model.

Does the Entertainment Pod include a kitchen?

A kitchenette unit is available as an add-on rather than included as standard — the base model comes with full electrical wiring, and a kitchenette can be specified for a wet bar or light catering setup.

What is the Entertainment Pod used for?

It’s built as a dedicated backyard entertainment space — commonly used as a braai room, bar area, or games room, separate from the main house.
