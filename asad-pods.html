Before you build

Planning & approvals.

Steel-framed pods are regulated under South Africa’s National Building Regulations and local municipal rules. ASAD handles the build; here’s how approval works:

  1. Site assessment — we check zoning, building lines and title deed restrictions before you order.
  2. Architectural drawings — available from ASAD for an additional fee.
  3. Engineering certification — available from ASAD for an additional fee.
  4. Municipal submission — you submit the plans and pay the council fees, and follow up on approval.
  5. Council approval — we help you address any requested changes.
  6. Construction and inspections — we build only once approvals are in place, and arrange inspections and certificates.

Fees, timelines and requirements vary by municipality. Some small secondary dwellings may qualify for simplified approval. Plans must be approved before construction where the law requires it.

How much does an ASAD pod cost?

From R45 000 for a backyard office or guard house, up to R1 095 000 for the 3 Bedroom Pod. Every pod is custom-sized to your site.

Do I need planning permission for a pod?

It depends on your municipality, the pod’s size, and whether it’s permanent. Some small secondary dwellings qualify for simplified approval.

Is a steel pod better than a shipping container conversion?

For most residential and hospitality uses, yes — better insulation, more natural light, and a layout that isn’t fixed by a container’s shape.

Can an ASAD pod generate rental income?

Yes, through long-term letting or short-term platforms like Airbnb. See our Airbnb Guest Suite Pod.
