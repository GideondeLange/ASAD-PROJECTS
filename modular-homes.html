The difference

Modular, prefab and LSF.

A modular or prefab home is designed and built to a plan, then delivered and installed on your site, rather than built brick by brick where it stands. ASAD builds its homes on an engineered light steel frame (LSF) finished in timber.

  • 01
    Engineered LSF structure
    The light steel frame carries a minimum 30-year structure guarantee, and every home comes with an engineer’s certificate.
  • 02
    Fully fitted
    Kitchen, bathroom, plumbing and full electrical installation, with aluminium doors and windows throughout, are part of the build.
  • 03
    Custom designs and sizes
    Every home can be adjusted to your site and needs, and finishes are confirmed on quotation.
  • 04
    Add what you need
    Decking, a pergola, solar, a septic tank, a back-up water system, a hot tub, a fireplace or aircon can be added. See options and prices.
Good to know

Modular home questions.

How much does a modular home cost in South Africa?

ASAD modular homes start from R426 000 excl. VAT for the 40 m² 1 Bedroom Living Unit, R980 000 excl. VAT for the 85 m² 2 Bedroom Pod, and R1 295 000 excl. VAT for the 140 m² 3 Bedroom Pitched House.

What is the difference between a modular home and a prefab home?

Both are built to a design away from the final site and then installed there. ASAD’s homes are built on an engineered light steel frame (LSF) and finished in timber, then delivered and installed on your site.

Do I need planning permission?

Yes, in most cases. Plans must be approved by your municipality before construction where the law requires it. ASAD can supply architectural drawings and engineering certification for an additional fee; you submit the plans and pay the council fees.

Is there a guarantee on an ASAD modular home?

Yes. The light steel frame carries a minimum 30-year structure guarantee, and every home comes with an engineer’s certificate.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery and installation are included within 100 km of our Boksburg branch. Beyond that, transport is quoted separately.
